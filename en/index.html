About

A family optician in the heart of Novi Sad

Optika Ginter was founded by Bojana and Ervin. After many years working in the city's optical stores, they opened their own with one goal: to give people in Novi Sad quality glasses at a fair price.

We put our hearts into our work and do everything we can so our clients leave happy and take good care of their eyes.

Services & prices

Everything for your eyes

Prices are a guide. You get the exact price for your glasses in store, after your prescription check.

We check your prescription on the spot — no doctor's prescription needed. We recommend booking ahead so the optometrist can give you their full attention.
Lenses usually arrive within 1–5 days, and glasses are most often ready the next day. Fitting into one of our frames is free. Fitting lenses into your own frame: 500 RSD (client frames are accepted without warranty).
Astigmatism, prismatic correction, progressive lenses — we select and make glasses of any complexity. Delivery of complex lenses can take a little longer.
Air Optix in stock, and we order any lens available in Serbia. We'll help you learn to put lenses in and take them out.
We make a child's first glasses from a paediatric ophthalmologist's prescription. Lenses from 2,000 RSD per pair.
INVU, Romeo, and Ray-Ban coming soon.
Free for our clients: nose pad replacement and frame adjustment. Lens fitting comes with a 1-month warranty.

Payment: cash, card, cheques, instalments, bank transfer to our company account.
