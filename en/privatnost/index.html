Privacy policy

Who processes the data

The data controller is Optika Ginter, Trg Republike 25, 21000 Novi Sad, tax ID (PIB) 111512445, registration no. (MB) 65466635. For any questions about your data, write to optikaginter@yahoo.com or call 021 383 2131.

What we collect

When you reserve a frame on the website: first name, last name, phone number, the selected model and the time of the reservation. We do not use cookies or advertising tools for reservations.

Why we use it

Only to call you, confirm that the model is available and arrange a fitting in store. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising messages.

Legal basis

Your consent, given by ticking the box in the reservation form (Serbian Law on Personal Data Protection, Article 12(1)(1)). You may withdraw consent at any time; this does not affect processing carried out before withdrawal.

Where it is stored and who sees it

Data is kept in a protected database on Supabase servers in the European Union (Frankfurt). Only Optika Ginter staff have access. A notification about a new reservation (name, phone, model) is sent to staff via the Telegram app.

How long we keep it

Up to 12 months from the reservation. If you buy the frame, your details are transferred to the store's customer records and kept for as long as the law requires.

Your rights

You may request access to your data, correction, deletion or restriction of processing, and withdraw your consent — just write or call us. If you believe your rights have been infringed, you may lodge a complaint with the Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection (poverenik.rs).
